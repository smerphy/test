# Praetor

Praetor is a runtime policy enforcement layer for AI agents. It sits
between an agent and its tools, evaluates a declarative policy on
every tool call, and returns one of four decisions:

- `allow` — pass the call through unchanged
- `deny` — block the call; substitute a sentinel so the model can re-plan
- `transform` — rewrite the call's arguments (e.g. redact secrets)
- `require_approval` — pause until a human approves or denies

Every decision is written to a tamper-evident, Merkle-chained audit
log. The hosted control plane (commercial) adds policy authoring UI,
audit search, an approval inbox, and pre-built compliance report
bundles for NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act.

## Components

- **Engine** — the policy AST, evaluator, YAML parser, and CLI.
  Apache 2.0. Sub-5ms p99 evaluation on a 100-policy bundle.
- **SDK** — Anthropic & OpenAI middleware, blocking approval flow,
  streaming audit log with at-least-once shipping.
- **Control plane** — FastAPI service for ingestion, search, approvals,
  reports. Backed by Postgres.
- **Web UI** — Next.js 15 app for everything humans touch.

## Why

Mid-market and enterprise teams running agents in regulated industries
(finserv, healthcare, insurance, legal) need to prove — to their CISO,
their regulator, and their auditor — that the model can't do anything
their policy doesn't permit. Praetor turns "trust me" into a signed,
queryable, framework-mapped log.
